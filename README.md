# Hi, I'm Mohammad Hasan 👋

**Mathematician · Math Educator · Author · Data Science & Machine Learning enthusiast**

I completed my **B.Sc. (Honours) and M.S. in Mathematics** at the **University of Dhaka**.
For many years I have taught mathematics to SSC, HSC and university (Honours) students, and I run
**Job Math**, a platform that prepares candidates for the math part of government and bank job exams in Bangladesh.

I love the meeting point of **mathematics and code**: machine learning, deep learning and data science
are built on the math I teach every day, so I write books that teach both, in Bangla.

🌐 **Website:** [mathbookbd.com](https://mathbookbd.com)
